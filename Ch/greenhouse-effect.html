01 — Greenhouse Effect

How the Earth Stays Warm

The atmosphere surrounding Earth acts like a giant insulating blanket — trapping just enough heat to support life on the surface.

1

☀️ Solar Radiation Arrives

The Sun sends energy to Earth as short wavelength radiation, which passes easily through the atmosphere and hits the ground.

2

🌍 Earth Absorbs & Re-emits

The ground absorbs this energy, heats up, then re-emits it as long wavelength radiation (infrared heat) back upward.

3

🌫️ Greenhouse Gases Trap Heat

Greenhouse gases absorb this long-wave radiation and scatter it in all directions — including back toward Earth, keeping the surface warm.

💧
Water Vapour

Naturally present in the atmosphere — one of the most abundant greenhouse gases, playing a key role in the natural cycle.

🏭
Carbon Dioxide (CO₂)

Produced by breathing, burning, and decomposition. The primary gas amplified by human industrial activity.

🐄
Methane (CH₄)

A potent greenhouse gas released by livestock digestion and waste decomposition in landfills.

02 — Human Causes

Why Levels Are Rising

Human activities are pumping these gases into the atmosphere at unprecedented rates, turbocharging the natural greenhouse effect.

CO₂

Carbon Dioxide Sources

  • Burning fossil fuels (coal, oil, gas) releases carbon that was stored underground for millions of years
  • Deforestation removes trees that normally absorb CO₂ through photosynthesis, reducing Earth's carbon sink
CH₄

Methane Sources

  • Farm animals (like cattle) release methane as a byproduct of their digestive process
  • Waste in landfills is broken down by microorganisms which produce methane during decomposition
03 — Consequences

The Resulting Climate Change

It's important to distinguish between two related — but different — terms before exploring the impacts.

🌡️ Global Warming

The rise in Earth's average surface temperature caused by the enhanced greenhouse effect.

🌀 Climate Change

The long-term shifts in weather patterns that result from global warming — a broader, more complex outcome.

🌪️ 🌪️

Extreme Weather Events

Increased frequency and severity of droughts, hurricanes, and floods as atmospheric energy levels rise.

🌊 🌊

Sea-Level Rise

Caused by melting ice caps and glaciers, combined with the thermal expansion of warming seawater, threatening coastal communities.

🦋 🦋

Biodiversity Loss

Species unable to survive, adapt, or migrate fast enough as rainfall and temperature patterns shift dramatically across habitats.
