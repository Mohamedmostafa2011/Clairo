⚗️ Thermochemistry Guide

🌟 The Golden Rules

Bond Breaking requires energy → Endothermic (+)
Bond Making releases energy → Exothermic (-)
ΔH = Σ(Energy to Break Bonds) - Σ(Energy Released Making Bonds)
🔴
Endothermic
ΔH = Positive (+)
Absorbs energy from surroundings. More energy needed to break bonds than released when forming new ones. Feels cold to touch.

Examples:

  • Ice melting
  • Photosynthesis
  • Cooking an egg
  • Evaporation
🟢
Exothermic
ΔH = Negative (-)
Releases energy to surroundings. More energy released when forming bonds than needed to break them. Feels hot to touch.

Examples:

  • Combustion/Fire
  • Freezing water
  • Rusting iron
  • Respiration
